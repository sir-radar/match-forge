The owner canceled all five planned provider communications. MatchForge must
not send the drafted PitchAPI, API-Football, football-data.org,
Football-Data.co.uk, or Understat messages. It must not replace them with email,
dashboard, support, or public Reddit contact. The earlier StatsBomb inquiry was
already sent by the owner; it may receive a passive response, but MatchForge
must not follow it up.

Published provider terms may establish ordinary private-research permission
when they explicitly cover the intended access, automation, retention, and use.
Public availability alone is not permission. If a needed activity is not
clearly permitted, exclude that activity and record the exact restriction.

Further credentialed API requests require a separate, documented request
budget and explicit owner authorization. Existing sanitized probe evidence may
be used without repeating the calls. PitchAPI is the first technical research
priority. Its next review must use a predeclared procedure for complete-season
shot coverage, finite pre-shot xG, penalties, regulation periods, identifiers,
and cross-season xG-series consistency.

Isolated simulations are permitted only with appropriately authorized inputs
and must be labelled `EXPERIMENTAL_ONLY`. They cannot be presented as
Evaluation V2, promotion, production, or predictive-acceptance evidence. This
decision authorizes neither xG-for model implementation nor Evaluation V2.

Evaluation V2 remains blocked wherever the approved frozen requirements are
not met. Decision 1, Decision 2, thresholds, experiment budget, protected EPL
restrictions, immutable published forecasts, available baselines, and the
mandatory Rust simulation requirement remain unchanged. Understat and
Football-Data.co.uk are inactive fallbacks unless a specific documented gap
requires them.

This decision supersedes the unexecuted communication authority in
`AUTHORIZE_PROVIDER_CLARIFICATION_INQUIRIES_V1`; it does not alter that
historical decision record or prior research evidence.
