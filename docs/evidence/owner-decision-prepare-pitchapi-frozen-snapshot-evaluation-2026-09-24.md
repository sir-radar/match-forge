## Decision

For this private research project, the owner accepts PitchAPI retention and the
intended evaluation use as project assumptions. Provider-issued retention or
legal-use attestations are therefore not engineering admission gates for
`PITCHAPI_RETROSPECTIVE_EVALUATION_V1`. This is an owner risk decision, not a
claim that PitchAPI supplied legal advice, contractual rights, an historical
xG model identity, or a revision guarantee.

Prepare the separate PitchAPI protocol, its MatchForge-controlled immutable
snapshot and append-only revision design, observational compatibility gate,
corpus/firewall policy, acquisition budget, and decision package. Preserve the
StatsBomb-oriented `EVALUATION_V2` without amendment. Results from the two
protocols must retain different identities and must not be pooled.

spending, StatsBomb follow-up, or any change to Evaluation V2.
