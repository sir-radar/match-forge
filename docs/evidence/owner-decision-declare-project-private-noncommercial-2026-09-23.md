The repository owner declared that MatchForge is a private, non-commercial
research project and will not be distributed. Source qualification therefore
does not require commercial-use or redistribution rights.

This decision does not remove provider-specific requirements that apply to
private research. Terms review must still confirm permission for access,
automated or bulk acquisition, processing, and required retention. Attribution,
rate limits, credential controls, and other applicable usage restrictions remain
in force. Public visibility alone is not permission for automated or bulk
acquisition. Third-party source data must not be published unless its terms
permit publication.

Existing source-discovery and provider-alternative evidence remains unchanged
as historical evidence. Future source decisions may disregard conclusions based
only on missing commercial-use or redistribution rights, but must preserve all
data-quality, source-traceability, point-in-time, provider-identity, and model
authorization requirements.

This decision does not change Sprint 2's `FAIL`, Phase 3's `BLOCKED` status,
the protected Sprint 2 target firewall, Evaluation V2 authorization or corpus
status, phase gates, source quality requirements, model implementation authority,
promotion, or production scope.
